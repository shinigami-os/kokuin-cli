A compositor alone shows windows, but no clock, no launcher, no list of open
windows. The **desktop shell** provides them: Waybar or Quickshell around sway and
Hyprland, Plasma Shell for KDE, GNOME Shell (which is also the compositor).

kokuin takes the start of the shell as the sign that the desktop is there.
